About the Fest

Where ideas become ventures

PRAGYATHA '26 is the State Level Entrepreneurial Fest hosted by the ME-RIISE Foundation (A Section 8 Company) and the Technology Business Incubator at Malnad College of Engineering, Hassan. Over three days, students across the state come together to build, pitch, code, design and compete.

From 24-hour hackathons to pitching battles, stock-market workshops and build challenges — PRAGYATHA '26 is a celebration of innovation, creativity and excellence, with a ₹75,000 prize pool on the line.
