Our Start-Ups

We foster innovation and deliver solutions that drive progress. Our start-ups embody creativity and forward-thinking — building cutting-edge technology, empowering students and businesses, and creating lasting impact for tomorrow.

T

Tech@10

An idea to provide technical knowledge to budding students at a reasonable price — ten rupees per session, hence the name. We teach programming, Android and web development, making the skills needed for the corporate sector accessible and affordable.

Know more K

Tech with KGP

A simple but effective vision built on teaching, effective learning and problem solving. Driven by a passion for app development, TechWithKPG has done tremendous work providing courses and internships for students of Malnad College of Engineering.

Know more D

Developers Community

Started by Adarsh D and Manoj Kumar H R in 2020 to spread knowledge and develop an interest in industry-standard coding. The community built a web-based application for monitoring people under home quarantine during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Know more C

Chathurpravarthan Private Limited

Established in 2022, a remarkable success story. It originated as a start-up within Malnad College of Engineering, born out of the successful implementation of the "Solar Panel Cleaning Robot" project.

Visit website E

Edutechsphere

Where technology meets education to create transformative learning experiences. Our mission is to empower learners and educators with innovative tools and solutions that inspire growth, unlock potential and shape the future of education.
