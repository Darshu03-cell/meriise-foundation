About the Hackathon

Where campus ideas become national-level innovation

The Internal Smart India Hackathon 2026 is the on-campus qualifier hosted by the ME-RIISE Foundation at Malnad College of Engineering, Hassan. Student teams take on real problem statements, prototype under mentorship, and present before an expert jury — with the best teams advancing to represent the college at the national SIH.

  • Form your team & register

    Build a squad of innovators and lock in your problem statement.

  • Build with mentors

    Rapidly prototype your solution with guidance from industry & faculty mentors.

  • Pitch & qualify

    Present to the jury; top teams advance to the national Smart India Hackathon.

Roadmap

The hackathon journey

Five milestones from registration to the national stage.

Phase 01

Registration Opens

Teams form up, pick a problem statement and submit their entry.

Phase 02

Idea Submission & Screening

Teams pitch their initial idea; shortlisting for the build phase.

Phase 03

Mentorship & Build

Guided prototyping sessions with mentors from academia and industry.

Phase 04

Grand Finale & Jury

Live demos and pitches evaluated by an expert panel on campus.

Phase 05

Onward to National SIH

Winning teams represent MCE Hassan at the national Smart India Hackathon.
