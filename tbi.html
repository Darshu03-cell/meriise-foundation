About TBI-MCE

About the Incubator

Technology Business Incubator – Malnad College of Engineering (TBI-MCE), Hassan, managed by ME-RIISE FOUNDATION, is an initiative supported by the Department of Electronics IT, Bt, S&T, Government of Karnataka through the Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society (KITS) to foster innovation, entrepreneurship, and technology-driven startups.

₹10 Crore Grant · 2025–2030

TBI-MCE has been sanctioned a grant of ₹10 Crore for a period of five years from 2025-2030. The incubator provides state-of-the-art infrastructure, prototyping facilities, technical and business mentoring, seed funding, industry connections, and investor networking opportunities.

Seed Funding up to ₹9 Lakh

With a dedicated incubation space, TBI-MCE aims to nurture innovative ideas into commercially viable ventures, supporting student innovators, faculty entrepreneurs, researchers, and startups. Eligible startups can receive seed funding support of up to ₹9 Lakh per startup, subject to the programme guidelines and selection criteria.

Our Commitment

ME-RIISE FOUNDATION through TBI-MCE is committed to strengthen the regional startup ecosystem by promoting innovation, accelerating technology commercialization, and enabling sustainable entrepreneurship.
